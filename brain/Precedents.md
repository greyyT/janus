# Precedents

Accepted reusable judgment: how the user decides a recurring kind of question. Every entry under `## Active` may be applied without asking when all its `when` conditions hold and no `unless` applies. A precedent never authorizes a grant-gated action; see `brain/Grants.md`. The `decisions` skill owns how entries are proposed, accepted, refined, and retired.

## Entry format

```md
### Short title · P-NNN

- scope: global | project: Project Title
- when: every condition that must hold
- choose: the choice
- unless: conditions that void it
- because: the reason the user gave or approved
- evidence: [[tickets/J-NNN-slug|Ticket title (J-NNN)]] YYYY-MM-DD; …
- approved: YYYY-MM-DD
```
